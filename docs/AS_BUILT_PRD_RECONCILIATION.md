## Authentication — FINAL

- Internal login uses username and PIN.
- Initial PIN is `123456`; the model stores it through the hashed cast, never as plaintext.
- Accounts with `must_change_pin = true` may only use the first-PIN-change flow and logout until the PIN is changed.
- Inactive accounts cannot enter internal routes.
- PIN reset is a Super Admin account action according to the active employee-account flow.
- Guests may access public pages and login only.
- Internal routes require authentication and an active account.
- Capability is derived from the authenticated account role and the module-specific access helper.

**Evidence:** `routes/auth.php`, `routes/web.php`, `AuthenticatedSessionController`, `EnsurePinIsChanged`, `EnsureUserIsActive`, `docs/GLOBAL/ACCESS_CONTROL.md`.

## Employee / Kelola Karyawan — FINAL

- All active roles can view the company-wide employee list and basic detail.
- Super Admin can create, update, delete, process exit, manage accounts, manage organization masters, export, and view sensitive files.
- Admin/User are read-only.
- Sensitive fields excluded from Admin/User payloads are NIK, address, marital status, KTP/path, and private employee signature.
- Phone number is **not** a sensitive field and may be visible to Super Admin/Admin/User according to the active implementation.
- KTP remains on local/private storage and is delivered through a protected route.
- New employee signatures use local/private storage and a protected route; legacy read fallback may remain.
- Canonical exit route is `dashboard.karyawan.exit`; it disables the related account according to the active action.
- Employee export is Super Admin only.
- Existing username must not be regenerated by reseeding or account updates when an existing account is retained.

**Evidence:** `EmployeeController`, `ProcessEmployeeExit`, `routes/web.php`, `docs/DASHBOARD/EMPLOYEE/PRD.md`, `docs/DASHBOARD/EMPLOYEE/PERMISSIONS.md`.

## Employee Account — FINAL

- Canonical roles are `super_admin`, `admin`, and `user`.
- New account role is resolved server-side from `jabatan.role_id`.
- The browser cannot submit an arbitrary role.
- A Jabatan without `role_id` rejects account creation with a configuration error.
- Initial PIN is supplied as plaintext to Eloquent and persisted through the model hashed cast.
- Existing username, PIN, and active state are preserved unless a separate account action changes them.
- When an employee's Jabatan changes, `users.role_id` must synchronize to the new `jabatan.role_id` without changing username, PIN, or active state.
- The former `ResolveEmployeeAccountRole` architecture is obsolete.

**Implementation gap to document:** verify whether the current Jabatan update action performs existing-account role synchronization. Do not infer completion from the requirement alone.

## KPI — AS-BUILT / FORMALIZATION REQUIRED

The former KPI PRD statement that KPI was not implemented is obsolete. KPI is active in the codebase. The following is the current as-built baseline; any item marked partial/confirmation must not be presented as a fully approved business rule without review.

### Period model

- Calendar active period and selected/working period are separate concepts.
- Super Admin may retain a preparation period as the selected context.
- Non-Super Admin period selectors exclude preparation periods.
- Preparation period remains visible to Super Admin across KPI modules.
- Kinerja OPS may manage preparation parameters.
- KI, Daily, MPA/Monthly, and Nilai Akhir render preparation locked state rather than silently falling back to another period.
- Normal KI/K-OPS entry starts at the last calendar day of the period month at 00:00 and ends on the first day of the next month at 23:59:59.
- `KpiClock` is the business clock for KPI decisions.

### Daily Report

- Owner, authorized supervisor/subordinate scope, and Super Admin scope are enforced server-side.
- Today and yesterday are the normal editable window according to active implementation.
- Evidence uses protected record-based delivery; new evidence is private.
- Approval source distinguishes direct supervisor and Super Admin takeover.

### Kinerja Individu

- Employee submits values in the 1–100 range.
- Initial submit stores values, creates employee logical signature atomically, and locks values.
- Supervisor logical signature is independent approval and gates normal publication to Nilai Akhir.
- Employee submit alone does not publish normal KI to Nilai Akhir.
- If the employee misses the deadline, the record becomes not-filled/auto-submitted, locked, and effective value is 0.
- Super Admin correction of a normally submitted record makes corrected/effective score immediate, keeps value locked, invalidates the old signature revision, and reopens employee/supervisor acknowledgement signatures.
- Correction of a not-filled record uses Super Admin takeover for both logical slots and becomes Dialihkan.
- Logical signer and actual signer remain separate.

### Kinerja OPS

- Employee result is locked after submit/auto-submit.
- Normal result is published to Nilai Akhir only after employee and supervisor logical signatures are fulfilled.
- Corrected result is recalculated server-side from stored parameters and becomes effective immediately.
- K-OPS correction may change result values and correction reason, not parameter structure.
- New evidence and signature snapshots are private.

### MPA and Monthly

- MPA starts after HRD/attendance initial data is ready for the participant.
- Assigned evaluator may complete evaluator stage; Super Admin may assist/take over according to timing and capability.
- Normal evaluator completion is Sudah Dinilai; overdue Super Admin takeover is Dialihkan.
- Finalizing MPA creates the HRD Monthly logical signature using the actual finalizer.
- Monthly has four logical slots: HRD, employee, direct supervisor, and second supervisor.
- Employee and direct supervisor signatures are independent.
- If no second supervisor snapshot exists, Super Admin may fulfill that logical slot.
- All required Monthly signatures are the gate before MPA/attendance values are published to Nilai Akhir.

### Nilai Akhir

- Corrected effective KI/K-OPS scores are consumed immediately.
- Normal KI/K-OPS submissions are not published before required supervisor approval.
- MPA/attendance values are not published before the required Monthly signatures.
- Effective score, status, category, and export use the current effective revision.
